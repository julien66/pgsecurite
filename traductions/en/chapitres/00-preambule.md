# Preface


This book is not a flying manual. It will not teach you how to inflate a wing, how to core a thermal or how to clear a cravat. Excellent books already do that.

It is a look at our community and at the way it thinks about safety. I write it from where I stand as a pilot, as a national coach and as a federation accident investigator. I also write it as a friend of those who have fallen. I have the feeling that our community is on the wrong track. I want to share observations, hypotheses and proposals, in the hope that they will open the discussion and that, perhaps, our ways of thinking will change.

Some passages may offend. I am well aware of that. I did not write this book against pilots, instructors, organisers or institutions. I am fighting ideas that cost us lives and, tired of doing so on my own, I decided I had to make a book of it.

I hesitated, I took my time, but here it is. It goes to Benoit, Bram, Cécile, Dan, Daniel, Françoise, Franz, Innes, Jonathan, Marie-Christine, Mathias, Maud, Norman, Philippe, Rémi, Scotty, Stephan, Sylvain, Timo, Xavier, Yoann… It goes to those I have forgotten, from the height of my vanity and the lack of empathy that comes with 25 years of lucky flying. It goes to all the others, and to those who, sadly, will follow.
