# Part 2: Proposals for free flight

We have described the beliefs that allow our community to live with its accidents without looking them in the face. This second part proposes ways out. They follow a simple logic. First, welcome accidents and their victims, because nothing will change until we say what is happening to us. Next, build a better system, that is, add the organisational and technical slices missing from our Reason model. Four chapters are devoted to this: knowing our accidents, informing better, equipping and training, then monitoring, alerting and rescuing. Finally, separate tourist flying from independent flying, to remove the ambiguity on which our safety discourse rests. A last chapter proposes setting ourselves numerical targets, to know whether all this works.

I promised to respect the freedom of free flight. The table below therefore ranks the proposals according to what they cost the pilot. Most take nothing away from them: they inform them, connect them to others, and help them rescue and be rescued.

| What the proposal asks | Proposals |
| :---- | :---- |
| Know more and inform better | Accident mapping, a TEM reading of our scenarios, accident forms that also record the system, more precise GPS tracks (IGC2), the WAT scale, reference level, weather stations, safety weather bulletins, a list of victims |
| Stand together | Just culture, listening to critical voices, lessons learned (RETEX), a victim support association, monitoring and assistance, volunteer rescue |
| Get organised | Site equipment and labels, safety equipment and training for each type of flying, a charter rather than obligations, professional use of VHF, transparency on equipment |
| Support and supervise | A long-term sponsor and mentor for every trainee, a dedicated framework for tourist flying and passenger flights |

None of these ideas is final. They are proposals to be discussed, improved and tried out.
