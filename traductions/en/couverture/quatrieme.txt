# Is paragliding a safe sport, with risks that can be controlled?

That is what the free-flying community has been telling itself for forty years. Meanwhile, our friends keep falling, and every accident is closed with the same explanation: the pilot made a mistake.

This book invites us to consider other options. It takes apart the myths that reassure us, shows how proven safety tools have been bent so that nothing changes, and puts forward concrete proposals: welcoming our accidents rather than judging them, building together the protections we lack, and separating commercial leisure flying from personal commitment.

Without ever giving up what lies at the heart of free flight: its freedom.

> “We will go on flying at our own risk. Let us make sure we no longer face our perils alone.”

@ Julien Garcia has been flying since 2001. National coach of the French paragliding team, team world champions in 2023 and 2025, and holder of the French distance record, he has lost enough friends along the way to change the way he sees his sport and its safety.
