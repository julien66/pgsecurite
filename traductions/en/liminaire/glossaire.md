# Glossary {.unnumbered}

French acronyms are kept in their original form, as they appear in the text, with an English explanation.

ADS-B
:   *Automatic Dependent Surveillance–Broadcast*. System by which an aircraft broadcasts its position, used by commercial aviation and much of general aviation.

ADS-L
:   *ADS-Light*. Lightweight position-broadcasting standard, designed for inexpensive, low-power devices and promoted in light aviation and free flight.

AFNOR
:   *Association française de normalisation*. French standards body.

AIDA
:   Name of the accident database shared by the EHPU federations, developed by the DHV.

APPN
:   *Activités physiques de pleine nature*. Outdoor physical activities (a French category of sports).

ASN
:   *Appel sélectif numérique*. Digital Selective Calling (DSC): a marine radio function that sends a distress alert directly to rescue services and nearby vessels.

ASRS
:   *Aviation Safety Reporting System*. NASA system that has collected confidential incident reports from American pilots since 1976.

BASE
:   *Building, Antenna, Span, Earth*. Refers to parachute jumping from a fixed object.

BMS
:   *Bulletin météo de sécurité*. French marine weather warning, issued to sailors as soon as the forecast wind exceeds force 6 on the Beaufort scale.

CCC
:   *Competition Class Certification*. CIVL standard for competition wings.

CEN
:   European Committee for Standardization. Its technical committee TC 136 covers sports, and its working group WG 6 covers paragliding equipment.

CIVL
:   *Commission internationale de vol libre*. FAI Hang Gliding and Paragliding Commission.

CROSS
:   *Centre régional opérationnel de surveillance et de sauvetage*. French regional centre that coordinates rescue at sea.

CUDOS
:   Robert K. Merton's four norms of science: communalism, universalism, disinterestedness and organised scepticism.

DHV
:   *Deutscher Hängegleiterverband*. German hang gliding and paragliding federation.

EHPU
:   European Hang Gliding and Paragliding Union. European union of free-flight federations.

EN
:   European standard. Here, the certification of paragliders under the EN 926 standard.

EPI
:   *Équipement de protection individuelle*. Personal protective equipment (PPE).

EPS
:   *Éducation physique et sportive*. Physical education in French schools.

ETTO
:   *Efficiency-Thoroughness Trade-Off*. Trade-off between efficiency and thoroughness described by Erik Hollnagel.

FAI
:   *Fédération aéronautique internationale*. World Air Sports Federation.

FANET
:   *Flying Ad-hoc Network*. Open protocol for sharing position between aircraft. "FANET+" instruments combine it with FLARM.

FASST-CH
:   *Future Aviation Surveillance Services and Technologies in Switzerland*. Swiss plan for the electronic visibility of aircraft, including ADS-L.

FFVL
:   *Fédération française de vol libre*. French free-flight federation (paragliding, hang gliding, kites).

FLARM
:   Anti-collision system that broadcasts the aircraft's position and warns of collision risk.

FSVL
:   *Fédération suisse de vol libre*. Swiss free-flight federation (SHV in German).

GNSS
:   *Global Navigation Satellite System*. Generic term for satellite positioning systems, including GPS.

GPS
:   *Global Positioning System*. American satellite positioning system.

GSM
:   Mobile phone network.

IGC
:   International Gliding Commission, the FAI's gliding commission. Also the file format for flight tracks (.igc). IGC2 is the more precise format proposed in this book.

LOSA
:   *Line Operations Safety Audit*. Method for observing line flights, published by ICAO, which presents the TEM model.

LTF
:   *Lufttüchtigkeitsforderungen*. German paraglider certification, issued under the authority of the DHV.

MMSI
:   *Maritime Mobile Service Identity*. Unique identification number of a marine radio.

OACI
:   *Organisation de l'aviation civile internationale*. International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO).

OBL
:   *Organisme à but lucratif*. Profit-making organisation (French category for commercial flight schools and operators).

PDMC
:   *Paraglider Dead Man's Curve*. Diagram by Rick Masters that plots height against type of incident to show when it is too late to throw one's reserve parachute.

PGHM
:   *Peloton de gendarmerie de haute montagne*. French gendarmerie mountain rescue unit.

PIREP
:   *Pilot Report*. A pilot's report on conditions encountered in flight, shared with other airspace users.

PMR446
:   *Private Mobile Radio*, on the 446 MHz band. Consumer walkie-talkies that can be used without a licence.

PWCA
:   *Paragliding World Cup Association*. Organiser of the Paragliding World Cup.

RETEX
:   *Retour d'expérience*. Lessons learned, incident reporting and feedback.

SGS
:   *Système de gestion de la sécurité*. The FFVL's safety management system (SMS).

SIGMET
:   *Significant Meteorological Information*. Weather warning for aviation.

SIV
:   *Simulation d'incident de vol*. Course in which in-flight incidents are deliberately induced over water to learn how to manage them.

STAPS
:   *Sciences et techniques des activités physiques et sportives*. French university degree in sport science.

SUAPS
:   *Service universitaire des activités physiques et sportives*. French university sports service.

TEM
:   *Threat and Error Management*. Model from aviation.

TOT
:   *Time-Out Timer*. Setting that stops a radio's transmission after a set time.

UCPA
:   *Union nationale des centres sportifs de plein air*. French non-profit organisation running outdoor sports centres and holidays.

ULM
:   *Ultraléger motorisé*. Microlight aircraft.

UNSS
:   *Union nationale du sport scolaire*. French school sports federation.

VHF
:   *Very High Frequency*. Frequency band of the radios used in free flight.

VTT
:   *Vélo tout-terrain*. Mountain biking.

WAT
:   *Wind / Ascendance / Turbulence*. Descriptive scale of flying conditions proposed in this book.

WG6
:   *Working Group 6*. CEN working group responsible for paragliding equipment standards.
