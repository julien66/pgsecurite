# Acknowledgements {.unnumbered}

To Luc Armant, Maxime Bellemin, Mathilde Chivet, Serge Durrant, Alexia Fischer, Jean-Marc Galan, Thomas Senac and Louis Tapper, who read this book before publication and generously shared their feedback and impressions. Thank you for your time and your kindness: this book is a little better thanks to you.

To the French public sport service. It is my position as a state sports official (*cadre d'État*) that makes it possible for this kind of reflection on free flight to take shape. I am lucky to remain part of the free-flying community while being able to step outside it and look at it from a distance. The autonomy of the *professeur de sport* status is exceptional, and it helps free up the time and money needed for this step aside. Over time, this kind of expertise becomes more and more neutral and disinterested. That seems essential to me.

To the Fédération française de vol libre, to its leadership, and especially to its president, Clémence Petre, for her trust and for her long, hard road, in the pilots' interest, towards making international competitions safer.

To my boss and national technical director, Eric Wyss, who has been a constant source of intellectual inspiration over nearly 15 years of working together, as well as an example of expertise, competence, dedication and commitment to the community.

To my family, who support me in the best and worst moments all along the way. Thank you for understanding my choices and for sharing the risks with full awareness.
